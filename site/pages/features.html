features

two gestures,
everything else follows

One for a single file, one for several. Nothing else to learn.

rename a file

Change its extension in Finder. That is the whole gesture.

Suffix decides from the file's contents, never its name. A text file called notes.jpg is left alone. A PNG called photo.jpg is converted. Renaming between spellings — .jpg to .jpeg — does nothing, rather than needlessly re-compressing your photo.

images ⇄ imagesPNG, JPEG, TIFF, GIF, BMP, HEIC
anything → PDFone page per image
PDF → imagesmany pages arrive as a .zip
video ⇄ videoMOV, MP4, M4V — streams copied, not re-encoded
video → audiothe soundtrack on its own
documents → PDFPages and Keynote exactly; Word approximately

read the words in a picture

Rename a screenshot to .txt and you get the text that was in it.

This uses the Vision framework built into macOS — no service, no account, no upload. It also rescues scanned PDFs, which hold pictures of text and so return nothing from ordinary extraction: suffix reads the pages instead.

select several, press ⌥⌘S

Some things a filename cannot say. “Merge these three” has no name to type it into, and “compress this” needs a quality setting.

Those live in one panel, opened by the shortcut or by right-clicking and choosing suffix: file actions. The shortcut is yours to change in Settings.

merge into one PDF

PDFs and images, combined in the order you selected them, named after whatever they have in common.

compress

Smallest / balanced / best, plus a quality slider. The estimate is measured by actually compressing your first file — so it moves when you do.

create ZIP archive

Separate on purpose. “Compress” and “zip” are different verbs that English happens to spell the same.

The panel greys out what does not apply and says why, rather than the entry quietly not being there. A file that comes out larger than it started is discarded instead of handed to you as an improvement.

and when it goes wrong

undo, for seven days

The original is copied aside before anything is overwritten. Any conversion can be undone from the menu bar. Copies are deleted automatically after a week, rather than quietly becoming a second copy of your photo library.

replace, or keep both

Your choice, set during setup and changeable any time. Replace leaves one file, as renaming implies. Keep both leaves photo.pdf and photo.png side by side.
